About this gallery

The four pieces below are composite, anonymous exemplars built to show what a passing Voice for Change proposal looks like — they are not the work of real students, and no real names appear. Once the platform launches with cohorts, only submissions whose author has given explicit, withdrawable consent appear here, under a chosen pseudonym. ผลงานในตัวอย่างนี้สร้างขึ้นเพื่อแสดงเกณฑ์มาตรฐาน — เมื่อระบบเปิดใช้จริง จะปรากฏเฉพาะผลงานที่ผู้เขียนยินยอมเปิดเผยภายใต้นามแฝง

SDG 4 · West · Mae Sot

Fund the bridge first, then move us across

By Pseudonym · West Champion 2026
Addressed to · Tak Primary Educational Service Area Office 2 · สพป.ตาก เขต 2
I propose that the district commit ฿840,000 to fund two bridging teachers in each of the eight receiving Thai schools for one academic year, and ฿160,000 for shared Karen-Thai learning materials. I accept that this plan does not reach the over-15 cohort this cycle — that is a real cost. I propose addressing them in Year 2, once the bridging staff are proven to work for the 6–14 group. As I committed in the Mae Sot mission, enrolment is not inclusion; the bridge must exist before the calendar.

SDG 11 · Central · Bangkok

A Baan Mankong precondition for the corridor

By Pseudonym · Central Champion 2026
Addressed to · BMA Department of Drainage and Sewerage · สำนักการระบายน้ำ กรุงเทพมหานคร
I propose that the Bangkok Metropolitan Administration commit ฿1,000,000 toward the first phase of a Baan Mankong-style relocation settlement for the klong-side community on the corridor alignment — a co-designed land plot inside the same district, with secure tenure, before the first wall is poured. I accept this lengthens the timeline by one season. The community-network organiser was clear: doing consent last is what slows the corridor, not consent itself.

SDG 3 · East · EEC fringe

Supported อสม. rounds before a satellite clinic

By Pseudonym · East Champion 2026
Addressed to · Provincial Public Health Office, Rayong · สสจ.ระยอง
I propose that the Provincial Public Health Office fund a 12-month pilot in the three fringe villages with the highest unmet-need indicators: ฿600,000 for stipends and training for the existing อสม. volunteers (so the village system is no longer unpaid), and ฿400,000 for a twice-weekly satellite clinic. I accept this does not reach every fringe village in Year 1 — that is the trade-off — but it lets the cost-per-prevented-admission be tested honestly before scaling.

SDG 14 · South · Andaman

A tied transition fund for the smallest operators

By Pseudonym · South Champion 2026
Addressed to · Department of Marine and Coastal Resources · กรมทรัพยากรทางทะเลและชายฝั่ง (ทช.)
I propose that DMCR ringfence ฿1,000,000 of the next reef-recovery cycle as a transition fund tied to the closure window — ฿700,000 in low-season buoy-and-monitoring contracts for the 30 smallest single-boat operators along the affected reefs, ฿300,000 for community-cooperative training. I accept that the larger tour-companies receive no compensation in this proposal. That is deliberate. They can absorb the closure; the smallest operators cannot, and the cooperative leader was right that order is where the harm hides.

How submissions reach the Hall of Voices

ขั้นตอนการคัดเลือกผลงานเข้าหอเสียง

  1. The learner submits a Voice for Change. Their teacher receives the work in the admin grading queue and evaluates it with Rubric A — this is the official grade.
  2. Three AI judges add formative feedback immediately on the submitted page — for the learner's reference only. The teacher's grade is never affected by the AI critique.
  3. If the learner has consented, and the teacher considers the piece exemplary, the teacher proposes it for curation. A second teacher reviews. Without two-teacher agreement, the piece never appears here.
  4. The learner is contacted with the final pseudonym and excerpt that would appear publicly. They have 14 days to withdraw or revise. Default is no publication.
  5. Onward sharing to a Thai youth SDG forum (if the learner additionally opted in) requires a further explicit opt-in per forum. We never assume a blanket yes.
