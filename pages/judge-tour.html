01. Step 01 / 08 · Overview

The project in 60 seconds

ภาพรวมโครงการใน ๖๐ วินาที

An AI-scaffolded English-language learning platform for LALA109 at the Faculty of Liberal Arts, Mahidol University — six adaptive missions across Thailand, one Voice for Change capstone.

FUTUREPROOF replaces the legacy group-project component of LALA109 · English for Digital Communication Skills with a single-player learner journey that runs in the browser. Each learner traverses six SDG-anchored missions tied to a real Thai region — climate, water, cities, education, health, oceans — and clears them by passing an AI-scored five-stage mastery arc.

The AI handles scale and consistency (formative scoring at every decision, language coaching at every register, dossier delivery at three CEFR tiers). The teacher remains the summative grader via Rubric A on the final Voice for Change capstone. This is the AI-TPACK boundary made operational.

What you are about to walk through below is the same eight-step journey a real LALA109 learner takes — except step 4 (a full SDG 6 mission) is pre-completed so you can see what every stage looks like when filled out.

02. Step 02 / 08 · Onboarding

Sign-up, consent, diagnostic, avatar

การลงทะเบียน ขั้นตอนการยินยอม PDPA การวินิจฉัยระดับ CEFR และการสร้างอวตาร

Four screens — completed once per learner, in this exact order. PDPA consent is hard-gated: the diagnostic does not run and writes nothing until consent is recorded.

Every learner journey starts the same way:

  1. Sign-up — name, email, password, institution, year of study.
  2. PDPA consent — bilingual flow with granular flags: basic profile · photo · voice · decisions · public showcase. Each flag is separately revocable from the learner's My Data page.
  3. CEFR diagnostic — ~12 minutes. Calibrates the learner's reading tier (B1+ / B2 / C1). Audio is never tiered — only written dossier content adapts.
  4. Avatar creation — Stability AI-generated stylised portrait used on the dashboard and on the final certificate.

The whole flow takes about 18 minutes. After that, the learner enters the Thailand Journey — and never sees this onboarding flow again unless they choose to update consent.

PDPA — the load-bearing rule

  • Consent precedes any personal-data write
  • Five granular flags, separately revocable
  • "My Data" screen — self-service export & deletion
  • 90-day auto-deletion post-course
  • All admin access logged for audit
  • Encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest

03. Step 03 / 08 · The Thailand Journey

The Thailand Journey map

แผนที่ภารกิจประเทศไทย ๖ ภูมิภาค ๖ SDG

One country, six regions, six SDG-anchored tensions. Learners may take them in any order.

The mission-select screen is a hand-illustrated watercolour map of Thailand. Each of the six regional missions is pinned with an amber-glow marker. Earned Keystones light up gold; unearned ones remain ink-line. Clearing all six unlocks the Voice for Change capstone.

The six missions are not interchangeable — each is built on a specific Thai community tension with vetted institutional context, real stakeholder positions, and a defensible counter-argument. None of them is a "wrong answer" mission.

SDG 6 · Water

The Aquifer Below · Khon Kaen

Northeast · Nam Phong / Ubolratana watershed
Difficulty CEFR B1+ → B2 · Cycle 90 min · Reading tier (your demo): B1+ · Ethical axes: Distributive justice × Intergenerational equity
Brief Probe · Dossier Probe · Stakeholders Decide & Act Debrief

Stage 1 · BRIEF complete

Bloom — Remember / Understand

The opening brief introduces the regional tension in three short paragraphs and one sharp decision question. The learner reads, marks "I'm ready," and proceeds.

Briefing. Khon Kaen sits above the Khorat Plateau aquifer, a freshwater store shared with Laos. As dry seasons lengthen, more of the province's drinking water comes from underground. Deepening the municipal wellfield secures dry-season supply for around 200,000 residents — but accelerates aquifer drawdown the next generation will inherit. Decision point: should the province authorize an additional 12 deep wells this dry season, knowing recharge cannot keep pace with current extraction?

Stage 3 · DECIDE & ACT — six-item quiz 6 / 6 answered

Bloom — Evaluate & Apply · adaptive · confidence-trap on Q3

The quiz combines six question types (multiple-choice · rank · match · vocabulary · confidence calibration · open commitment) that force the learner to synthesise dossier + stakeholder positions and commit to a defensible decision under a real trade-off.

  1. Which institution holds the legal authority to issue the 12 new well permits?
    • PWA Khon Kaen branch
    • Department of Groundwater Resources (DGR) under MNRE
    • Royal Irrigation Department
    • Khon Kaen Provincial Council
  2. Saline intrusion is a problem because…
    • It raises the boiling point of water.
    • Once an aquifer turns saline, reversing the damage takes decades — locking in a quality outcome for future generations.
    • It increases the volume of available water.
    • It is a seasonal phenomenon that self-corrects in the monsoon.
  3. Rank these four sources by how much weight you would give each, in this order of strongest to weakest. (Confidence-trap item · overclaiming on weak evidence costs Insight Tokens)
    • KKU Groundwater Research Institute peer-reviewed brief
    • Department of Groundwater Resources statutory data
    • Industry-association policy note (declared conflict of interest)
    • Anecdotal household reports from a single village
  4. Match the stakeholder to the concern they most clearly own:
    • Smallholder farmer → upper-aquifer drawdown affecting shallow private wells
    • PWA Operations Manager → continuous tap-water supply mandate for 200,000 residents
    • Food-processing Director → existing deep-aquifer permit issued under separate regulation
    • Community Health Volunteer · อสม. → rise in childhood diarrhoea when households switch to surface water
  5. Define intergenerational equity as it applies to this scenario, in one sentence.
    • Sample C1 response: "Allocation decisions made today must not foreclose freshwater quality and quantity for a generation that has no voice in the present decision — drawdown that accelerates salinity intrusion is a borrowed cost from the future."
  6. Commit to one piece of evidence you will carry into the Voice for Change capstone — and name the trade-off you accept.
    • Sample commitment: "I will carry the GWRI saline-intrusion projection. The trade-off I accept: tighter industrial-permit caps in exchange for protected upper-aquifer recharge, knowing some food-processing facilities will lose capacity."

05. Step 05 / 08 · The capstone

The Voice for Change capstone

ภารกิจปิดท้าย · ข้อเสนอเสียงเพื่อการเปลี่ยนแปลง

Six Keystones unlocks one screen: the learner records a 2-to-5 minute proposal addressed to a real Thai institution, in any one of three submission lanes. The teacher grades this. AI does not.

The Voice for Change prompt is constant across all learners:

"Having walked all six frontlines, propose one concrete action for a Thai community — addressed to a real audience, not the teacher."

Three submission lanes (CAST UDL · Multiple Means of Action and Expression):

  • Record live — in-browser, 60–180 s, unlimited re-record
  • Upload — a prepared audio or short video file
  • Create with Canva — opens a template in a new tab, exported and re-uploaded

The lane is a means of expression — it never changes the bar. Rubric A (teacher-graded, summative) scores five dimensions × 1.0/0.5 weighting · max 45 · bands 38/30/22.

Why this is human-graded only

  • The AI-TPACK boundary — made operational
  • Formative scoring (Rubric B) is AI · summative grade (Rubric A) is human
  • Cultural authenticity of the address stays with the teacher
  • The judge of the learner's voice is a voice itself

06. Step 06 / 08 · After submission

Certificate, badge, AI Judges' critique

ใบรับรอง · ตราสัญลักษณ์ · คำวิจารณ์เชิงพัฒนาโดย AI Judges สามท่าน

After submitting, the learner sees three artefacts at once: their downloadable certificate, their hexagonal badge, and the formative critique of all three AI Judges. The teacher's Rubric A grade arrives separately.

The submitted screen is built around three things:

  1. Certificate of Completion — a dark editorial-style document with the learner's name, six Keystones, programme-director signature line, and unique credential ID. Downloadable as PNG or PDF.
  2. Hexagonal badge — a portfolio-ready credential the learner can drop on LinkedIn or a CV.
  3. AI Judges' Initial Critique — three personas (Dr. Anchalee · Khun Nattaphum · Khun Suthida), each scoring a dimension and surfacing a strength plus a growth edge. Formative only. Always advisory. Never overrides the teacher.

A bottom banner makes the boundary explicit: "Your teacher receives your submission in the admin grading queue and evaluates it with Rubric A — this is the official, final grade."

The three AI Judges (formative · advisory)

  • Dr. Anchalee Suwannapong — language proficiency & register
  • Khun Nattaphum Boonkrong — argumentation & counter-position
  • Khun Suthida Phetcharat — community address & ethical reasoning
  • Each return must include a specific growthEdge
  • Random 5% audited against teacher score — drift > 0.5 triggers prompt revision

07. Step 07 / 08 · Public gallery

The Hall of Voices

หอเสียง · แกลเลอรีสาธารณะของผลงานข้อเสนอ Voice for Change

Voices that earned their six Keystones and chose — via explicit, withdrawable consent — to share their proposals beyond the classroom.

The Hall of Voices is the platform's Community of Practice in the Lave & Wenger sense — learners enter as peripheral participants (reading others' submissions) and may graduate to full participants (their own work featured). Curation is governed by consent at every step.

The four pieces currently on display are composite, anonymous exemplars — built to show what a passing proposal looks like. They are not the work of real students. When the platform launches with cohorts in semester 1 of academic year 2569, only submissions whose author has given explicit, withdrawable consent will appear here, under a chosen pseudonym.

Curation rules

  • Author must opt in separately from PDPA basic consent
  • Pseudonym, not real name
  • Withdrawal removes the entry within 7 days
  • Admin curator reviews submissions before featuring
  • Best pieces may be forwarded to Thai SDG youth forums (further opt-in required)

08. Step 08 / 08 · The teacher side

The Admin Console

แดชบอร์ดผู้ดูแลระบบ · ๗ โมดูล สำหรับครูผู้สอน

Seven modules for the teacher — Learners · Missions · Analytics · Moderation · PDPA Center · Hall of Voices Curator · System. The teacher's grade is always the authoritative one; AI Judge scores are advisory only.

The Admin Console is gated behind Firebase Auth with an allowlist. The teacher signs in once per term and sees the full cohort:

  • Learners — roster · profile edit · keystone grant/revoke · PDPA deletion
  • Missions on/off — toggle availability per region for special cohorts
  • Analytics — engagement · region completion · 5-stage decision matrix · CEFR delta
  • Moderation — flagged AI judgements · replay against rubric · calibration drift audit
  • PDPA Center — consent overview · DSAR queue · admin audit log · 90-day purge schedule
  • Hall of Voices Curator — feature / unfeature submissions · withdrawal handler
  • System — demo-mode toggle · pass threshold · model selection

Who can access
