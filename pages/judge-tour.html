Welcome to the Judge Tour

ขอต้อนรับคณะกรรมการสู่การสาธิตแพลตฟอร์ม FUTUREPROOF

A guided 60-second entry into FUTUREPROOF — SDGs Mission Journey, an AI-scaffolded English-language learning platform built for LALA109 (Faculty of Liberal Arts, Mahidol University). The five cards below give you the fastest possible read on what the platform does, how it works, and what supports it pedagogically. Start with the demo journey if you have ten minutes — start with the Hall of Voices if you have two.

What you are about to see in 60 seconds

  • Six adaptive missions across Thailand (one per SDG: 3, 4, 6, 11, 13, 14) each built on a real Thai community tension — water, climate, cities, education, health, oceans.
  • Every mission runs the same five-stage arc (BRIEF → PROBE → DECIDE → ACT → DEBRIEF), scored by three AI Judges against an auditable rubric.
  • Clear all six and you unlock Voice for Change — a multimodal capstone proposal addressed to a real Thai institution. The teacher grades this; AI does not.
  • Throughout, Mr Compass (Socratic AI Field Mentor) offers language support only, never content answers — enforcing the AI-TPACK boundary at the prompt level.
  • The Hall of Voices is a curated public gallery of capstone exemplars — your fastest path to seeing the bar this platform sets.

Load-bearing framework

PICRAT

Every mission lives at Interactive-Amplifies or above. Capstone reaches Creative-Transforms (Kimmons et al. 2020).

Load-bearing framework

AI-TPACK

The boundary between what AI handles (scale, consistency) and what humans handle (authority, cultural judgement) — made auditable.

Three theories, operationalised

Behaviorism · Cognitivism · Social Constructivism

Each mapped to specific platform features — not cited decoratively.

Compliance from day one

PDPA · UDL 3.0 · WCAG 2.1 AA

Bilingual consent, granular flags, 90-day auto-deletion, multi-modal access, mobile-first from 360 px.
