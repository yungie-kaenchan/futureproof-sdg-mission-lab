Getting startedSection 01

What is FUTUREPROOF: SDGs Mission Journey?

FUTUREPROOF is an AI-guided learning platform where you take on real Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) challenges across six regions of Thailand. Each mission asks you to investigate a real-world trade-off, weigh stakeholder voices, decide under uncertainty, and defend your reasoning in English.

You'll build English fluency, analytical thinking and ethical judgment together — not as separate subjects.

How do I sign up?

From the home page, click Begin your journey and complete the sign-up form. You'll be asked for your email, a password, your name in English and Thai, and a few demographic details (age, gender, institution, year of study, province).

After sign-up you'll be guided through PDPA consent → a short readiness check → your avatar → the Thailand journey map.

Do I need to install anything?

No. FUTUREPROOF runs entirely in your browser. Chrome, Edge, Safari, and Firefox all work. Mobile is supported but a tablet or laptop is more comfortable for the reading-heavy stages.

How long does one mission take?

About 60–90 minutes for the full five-stage arc (Brief · Probe · Decide · Act · Debrief). You don't have to finish in one sitting — every stage auto-saves, and you can Save for later any time. When you come back, click Resume on the welcome banner.

What's the readiness check at the start?

A brief, 8–10 minute diagnostic in four sections (language, critical thinking, analytical thinking, collaboration). It's not graded — its only job is to calibrate the missions to your reading level so they're challenging without being overwhelming.

MissionsSection 02

What are the six missions?

Each mission is a real SDG trade-off in a Thai region:

SDG 6 · Northeast / Khon Kaen — The Aquifer Below
SDG 13 · North / Chiang Mai — The Burning Season
SDG 11 · Central / Bangkok — The Klong and the City
SDG 14 · South / Andaman — The Reef and the Tide
SDG 4 · West / Mae Sot — The Children at the Border
SDG 3 · East / EEC fringe — The Village the Boom Left Behind

You can take them in any order.

What are the five stages in each mission?

Every mission runs the same arc — different verbs, same shape:

① Brief (อ่านพื้นที่) — read the land, absorb the trade-off
② Probe (ขุดความจริง) — question the sources, weigh stakeholders
③ Decide (ฟันธง) — choose under pressure, calibrate your confidence
④ Act (ลงมือสื่อสาร) — communicate the decision to those it lands on
⑤ Debrief (ทบทวนผล) — reflect on consequence, earn the Keystone

Can I redo a mission?

Yes. On the journey map you can replay any region. If you pass again, you keep the Keystone (it's idempotent — you can't double-count). If you fail, your previous pass is preserved.

Within a single attempt, the Decide and Act stages let you retry once after seeing AI feedback.

What if I'm stuck on a question?

Three lifelines: (1) open Mr. Compass bottom-right for Socratic questions and English help, (2) re-read the dossier with the View dossier button bottom-left of the play area, (3) take a break and Save for later — your work is kept across devices.

Are missions in Thai or English?

English primary, Thai metalanguage. Mission content (dossiers, decisions, AI evaluation) is in English. Thai supports navigation, key labels, and clarifying notes. You're learning to do consequential work in English — the missions are the practice ground.

Tokens & KeystonesSection 03

What are Insight Tokens?

Tokens are the process reward. You earn them for accurate intel, strong reasoning, and good language; you lose a small amount for weak arguments or factual errors. They're shown in your right-pane stats during the mission.

Tokens are spendable: in future versions you'll be able to use them in a shop for Expert Consultant questions, language-coach feedback, premium templates, and hints.

What is an SDG Keystone?

A Keystone is the narrative key you earn by passing a mission's bar. Each region awards one Keystone. They are not spendable, and they are binary — you either hold a Keystone or you don't.

Collect all six to unlock the Voice for Change capstone.

What score do I need to earn the Keystone?

Your composite score must reach 60%. The composite is a weighted average of all five stages: Brief 15% · Probe 25% · Decide 30% · Act 25% · Debrief 5%. The heaviest weight sits where the thinking matters most (Decide).

How does the AI Judge score my decisions?

It scores against a published rubric (1–5 per criterion) for analytical depth, evidence quality, language quality, and counter-argument awareness — and tells you why with quotes from your own work. The score is formative (a draft for your teacher), not the final official grade.

Where can I see my progress?

The right-pane stats show your tokens, your composite, and which stage you're on. The Keystones Collected strip below shows all six SDG regions and lights the ones you've earned. The journey map on the mission select page shows the same at the platform level.

Mr. Compass · the Field MentorSection 04

What is Mr. Compass?

Mr. Compass is your AI Socratic mentor, available bottom-right on every mission page. He'll ask reflective questions, help with English (vocabulary, grammar, register), and suggest which teammate's role might address your question.

What will Mr. Compass NOT do?

By design, Mr. Compass will not: give you mission answers, evaluate your decisions, predict consequences, reveal hidden information, or translate the dossier. He guides; he doesn't decide. (That's what makes him useful — not what limits him.)

What language does Mr. Compass speak?

English only. If you write to him in another language he'll ask you to try again in English. He'll match your CEFR level — simpler sentences for beginners, academic register for advanced.

How many questions can I ask?

Up to 10 per mission. The count is shown in the chat panel. When you reach the cap he'll suggest you lean on your team — your Research Analyst, Communications Director, or Ethics Officer will see angles he can't.

Will my conversations with Mr. Compass be private?

Conversations are logged for your teacher to review as part of formative assessment. They're stored in your private profile under PDPA rules and auto-deleted 90 days after the course ends.

Voice for ChangeSection 05

What is the Voice for Change capstone?

After you've collected all six SDG Keystones, you'll write or record a proposal for a project that could help a Thai community — as if you were pitching it to a UN funder. Built around concrete fields: who it helps, what problem, what action, what it costs, and which mission insights informed it.

Can I submit by writing or audio?

Both. You can write it, record live audio (transcribed in real time), or upload a pre-recorded file. UDL principle: multiple ways to demonstrate the same competency.

Who grades the Voice for Change?

Both an AI evaluator (criterion-by-criterion evidence + suggested score) and your teacher (the official final grade). The AI's job is to give your teacher a strong starting draft, not to replace their judgment.

What is the Hall of Voices?

A public gallery of exceptional Voice for Change pieces — only published with the learner's explicit consent. Some pieces also travel to real Thai youth SDG forums, with additional consent. It's an authentic audience beyond the classroom.

Privacy & PDPASection 06

What data does FUTUREPROOF collect about me?

Your sign-up information (name, age, gender, institution, year of study, province, location), your readiness-check results, the decisions you make in missions, your written and recorded responses, the AI feedback you receive, and — with separate consent — your photo and voice recordings.

See the full breakdown on the consent screen.

Where is my data stored?

On Firebase in the asia-southeast1 (Singapore) region. Encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is logged. Your private data is only readable by you and your teacher (administrator); your public profile (display name, institution) is visible to teammates and the Hall of Voices if you consent.

How long is my data kept?

Personal data is auto-deleted 90 days after the course ends. Audit logs are retained for 7 years per institutional policy.

Can I see or delete my data?

Yes — from your My Data panel you can review what's stored and request deletion at any time. Withdrawing required consents (Basic profile, Mission decisions) ends your enrollment.

Who is the Data Controller?

Faculty of Liberal Arts, Mahidol University. Contact: payungsak.kaenchan@gmail.com. Complaints may also be raised with the Personal Data Protection Committee of Thailand.

TechnicalSection 07

I lost progress / clicked refresh — is my work safe?

Yes. Every stage transition auto-saves to both your device and the cloud. When you reload you'll see "Welcome back — Resume / Start over". Click Resume to continue exactly where you left off.

Can I work across devices?

Yes. Progress is mirrored to your Firebase profile. Sign in from any device and your resume points are there.

The cartoon avatar won't generate.

The cartoon pass is limited to 3 attempts per account. After the cap, you can still upload a photo as your avatar — and you can always change it later from your profile.

I see "Sign-up is not switched on just yet" — what does that mean?

The platform's Firebase backend isn't connected for this build. Ask your teacher — they'll know when student onboarding goes live.

I changed my mind about a consent option.

You can flip optional consents (photo, voice, AI generation) on or off at any time from My Data. Required consents (Basic profile, Mission decisions) cannot be withdrawn while you're enrolled — withdrawing them ends your enrollment.

The page looks broken on my phone.

Hard-refresh (pull-to-refresh on mobile) to clear any cached version. If the issue persists, take a screenshot and email payungsak.kaenchan@gmail.com.
