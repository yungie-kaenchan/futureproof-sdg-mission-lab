UNDP Thailand โครงการพัฒนาแห่งสหประชาชาติ ประเทศไทย

Your team has been shortlisted for a Thailand SDG Catalyst Grant of one million baht.

You have, over the past weeks, walked six of the communities the Catalyst Grant prioritises this cycle: Khon Kaen, Chiang Mai, Bangkok, the Andaman coast, Mae Sot, and the EEC fringe. You have read their dossiers. You have heard their voices. You have decided under pressure.

The committee meets next Friday. They will not award the million baht to a wish, a slogan, or a sentiment. They will award it to one proposal that:

  1. Addresses a real Thai audience by name — not your teacher, not "the public."
  2. Builds on the lived evidence you committed to during the missions — not aspirational claims.
  3. Names the trade-off it deliberately accepts — because there is always one, and pretending otherwise fails the committee.
  4. Proposes one action — concrete, costed, sequenced — within the million-baht envelope.
  5. Speaks in language the audience can actually act on — not academic, not slogan, not pitch-deck.

You will not present in person. You will submit a recorded proposal — audio or video, 2 to 5 minutes — with a written transcript the committee can refer back to.

The grant is real money. The decision is real. The community is real. Speak as if you mean it. Because you do.

— Aj. Yungie Programme Director · FUTUREPROOF
