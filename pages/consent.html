Data Controller: Faculty of Liberal Arts, Mahidol University. Contact: payungsak.kaenchan@gmail.com.

Lawful basis: Performance of an educational task and informed consent under the Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (PDPA).

What we collect: Identifying information (name, email, institution, year of study), competency assessment results, decisions made during missions, written and recorded responses, AI-generated feedback you receive, and (with separate consent) your photo and voice recordings.

Your rights under PDPA: access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and withdrawal of consent. Exercise these from your My Data panel at any time.

If you withdraw a consent: the corresponding data is deleted. Required consents (Basic profile, Mission decisions) cannot be withdrawn while you're enrolled — withdrawing them ends your enrollment.

Children's data: the platform is intended for users 18+. Users under 20 may be subject to additional consent requirements per Thai law; we'll prompt for parental consent if applicable.

Complaints: direct to the Personal Data Protection Committee of Thailand or the Mahidol University DPO.
