You're about to cross Thailand — and become futureproof.

FUTUREPROOF is a guided journey across six Thai regions. Each region holds one real sustainable-development challenge. You'll investigate it, weigh hard trade-offs, act under pressure, and defend your reasoning — building English, critical thinking, and ethical judgement at the same time, because the platform never lets you separate them.

01

The journey: six regions, six Keystones

There's no fixed order. Open the Thailand map, choose any region, and take on its challenge. Meet the bar and you earn that region's SDG Keystone. Collect all six and your Voice for Change — the final task — unlocks.

SDG 6Northeast

The Aquifer Below Khon Kaen

A city secures its dry-season water — by drawing down the aquifer the next generation inherits.

SDG 13North

The Burning Season

Clean-air enforcement protects city lungs — and threatens upland farmers who burn to survive.

SDG 11Central

The Klong and the City

A flood plan shields 80,000 commuters — and displaces canal-side families who live on the water.

SDG 14South

The Reef and the Tide

A tourism quota lets the reef recover — and cuts the income of the divers who depend on it.

SDG 4West

The Children at the Border

Every child has a right to learn — where legal status and resources collide at Mae Sot.

SDG 3East

The Village the Boom Left Behind

The Eastern Economic Corridor pulls workers away — leaving elders and thinning healthcare behind.

03

What's expected of you

Honest effort, in English, with a teammate or two if you have them. The platform calibrates difficulty to you — so the demand is real but never crushing.

translate

English, primarily

All missions run in English. Thai appears for navigation, consent, and key reflection — never as a crutch that removes the challenge.

tune

A short readiness check

About 12–15 minutes. It sets your reading tier so texts meet you at the right level. It's calibration, not a grade — there's no pass/fail.

groups

Solo, pair, or trio

Teams of three take a professional role each and must share intel in English. Solo and pairs are fully supported — AI fills the empty seats.

04

How your work is evaluated

Four kinds of judgement, each doing what it does best. AI gives you instant, consistent feedback at scale. People make the judgements that must stay human — your teacher's grade is the final, official one.

Tier 1 · During every mission

AI feedback

Formative · real-time · consistent

Instant feedback on each decision and draft. It coaches and calibrates — it never sets your final grade.

Tier 2 · Mid-journey

Peer review

Cross-team · rubric-guided

You review another team and are reviewed back, building the metacognition that comes from judging others' reasoning.

Tier 3 · Final task

Teacher grade

Holistic · summative · authoritative

Your teacher grades the Voice for Change with a published rubric. This is the official result — the AI never overrides it.

verifiedFinal authority
Tier 4 · Showcase

Authentic audience

Public · optional forum

With consent, strong work reaches a real audience in the Hall of Voices — and may be routed to a youth SDG forum.

05

Two things you collect — and they're different

Don't confuse them. One you spend; one you can't. Keeping them separate is what keeps the journey honest.

toll

Insight Tokens

Spendable · earned per stage

Earn them for strong work; spend them on scaffolding — a language coach, an expert question, a hint. They reward process and ease the climb. They do not unlock the ending.

key

SDG Keystones

Not spendable · one per mission

One per region you pass — six in total. They can't be bought with Tokens. They are the only key to the Final Task: this protects the productive struggle.
