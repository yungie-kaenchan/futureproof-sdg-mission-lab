Step 3 · Hear from four stakeholders

Four stakeholders have recorded brief dispatches about the proposed wellfield expansion. Each speaks from inside their own role — a farmer, a provincial waterworks manager, an industrial production director, and a village health volunteer.

Watch the embedded video introduction below, then play each audio dispatch in any order. When all four are heard, the Continue button activates and you proceed to the adaptive quiz.
