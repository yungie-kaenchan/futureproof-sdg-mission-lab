PART 01

The Mission
Brief.

FUTUREPROOF reframes English language education as a vehicle for global citizenship. Students do not study English to pass tests. They use English to investigate real crises, deliberate over genuine ethical dilemmas, and produce professional artifacts of consequence — an experience inseparable from the language itself.

PILLAR 01

Adaptive Intelligence

i + 1

Each student begins with a competency diagnostic. The AI engine then generates every mission's content at precisely the level that stretches without overwhelming — Krashen's Input Hypothesis operationalized at scale.

PILLAR 02

Bloom's Architecture

06 / 06

Six missions mapped to six cognitive levels. Students progress from Remembering through Creating across a complete journey of intellectual ascent — each mission building structurally on what came before.

PILLAR 03

Authentic Output

∞

Students produce a Pitch Capsule — a professional, narrated, designed digital artifact addressing their chosen SDG crisis. It is portfolio-worthy, sharable, and stands as proof of integrated learning made visible.

PART 02

Six missions.
One ascent.

Each mission corresponds to a Bloom's cognitive level. Decisions branch the path. Performance shapes resources. No two team journeys are alike — but every path arrives at synthesis.

01
Remember

Recon

Intel gathering. Teams comb verified source materials, identify key facts, decode terminology, and map stakeholders. Strategic source selection shapes what knowledge they carry forward.

02
Understand

Decode

Comprehension under stakes. Teams must explain the crisis to a specific audience — minister, citizen, investor — forcing register shifting and audience awareness. Conflicting sources demand judgment.

03
Apply

Deploy

A live crisis event escalates the scenario. Teams must respond in real time — drafting communications, choosing strategy, and committing to action under uncertainty.

04
Analyze

Dissect

Consequences arrive. Teams break down what worked, what failed, and why — comparing approaches, mapping stakeholders, identifying root causes, and standing behind their analytical conclusions.

05
Evaluate

Tribunal

The ethical crucible. A moral dilemma generated from the team's specific journey. AI cross-examination probes for weakness. Students defend their reasoning under pressure — in English.

06
Create

Forge

The synthesis. Teams enter the Creation Studio to produce their Pitch Capsule — a designed, narrated, multi-panel digital artifact that crystallizes the entire journey into professional output.

PART 03

The Pitch
Capsule.

A five-panel professional artifact. Designed within the platform. Narrated by the team in their own voice. Exportable as PDF. Eligible for the Hall of Excellence.

Students do not write about a problem. They construct a piece of professional communication that demonstrates language proficiency in writing and speech, design judgment, ethical reasoning, and the discipline to synthesize a complex journey into a single deliverable.

01
Written English across multiple registers
02
Spoken English with AI pronunciation coaching
03
Visual design judgment & editorial coherence
04
Critical & ethical reasoning under pressure
05
Documented AI collaboration & authorship ethics
PITCH CAPSULE / 05 PANELS
Panel 01
The Crisis

Problem statement with data, sources cited, stakeholders named. Led by the Research Analyst — sets the why before the how.

Panel 02
The Journey

Decisions, missteps, lessons learned along the way. Collaborative authorship — the team's reasoning made visible.

Panel 03
The Insight

The single argument that survives cross-examination. Led by the Ethics & Policy Officer — clarity under pressure.

Panel 04
The Solution

Proposed action with stakeholder map and rollout. Led by the Communications Director — built to be read by power.

Panel 05 // Voice
The Voice

Recorded narration in your own voice over the whole capsule. Team-wide. Pronunciation coaching included before you press record.

PART 04

Four kinds
of judgment.

FUTUREPROOF deliberately separates what AI evaluates well from what only humans can. Each judgment layer does what it does best — and nothing more.

Tier 01

AI Judges

Real-time formative evaluation of decisions, language quality, factual accuracy, and structural coherence. Consistent. Scalable. Immediate.

Tier 02

Peer Judges

Cross-team review using rubrics. Builds metacognition. Sharpens the reviewer's own reasoning through evaluating others.

Tier 03

Teacher Judges

Holistic, summative, authoritative. Cultural wisdom. Recognition of authentic voice. Final professional grade. Irreplaceable.

Tier 04

External Judges

Authentic audience. Invited faculty, industry, NGOs at the Pitch Showcase. Awards beyond the classroom. Recognition that travels.
