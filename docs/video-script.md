# Video Demonstration Script — FUTUREPROOF: SDGs Mission Lab

**Target length:** 5–7 minutes
**Language:** English narration, Thai subtitles
**Recording set-up:** 1920×1080 screen capture, voice-over on a single audio track. Record the screen walk first, then layer the voice in second pass so timing is precise.
**Tone:** confident, editorial — never "promo-y." This is a Royal-Trophy academic submission, not a SaaS product launch.

## 1 · COLD OPEN (0:00 – 0:25)

**Visual:** Hero page top-of-fold. Brand wordmark animates in. SDG slider passes underneath in colored tiles. Then a quick cut to the operator silhouette on the staircase from the hero image.

**Narration:**

> Most language classrooms still ask one question: *did you understand the lesson?* This platform asks a different one — *can you act in the world, in English, on something that matters?*
>
> This is **FUTUREPROOF: SDGs Mission Lab** — an AI-powered gamified learning platform where undergraduate teams investigate the seventeen United Nations Sustainable Development Goals. In English. Through professional roles. Toward an artifact that travels.

---

## 2 · THE PROBLEM (0:25 – 1:00)

**Visual:** A short montage — a stack of grammar drills fading out, then an unfilled IELTS form, then the SDG tile constellation filling the screen. End on the hero title "Where English meets the world's most urgent challenges."

**Narration:**

> The problem isn't motivation. Thai undergraduates can pass the test and still cannot speak. They can produce the sentence and still cannot defend it. They can name "climate change" and still cannot tell you what it means for the Chao Phraya basin in 2034.
>
> The gap is what language education has historically separated — *form* from *consequence*. FUTUREPROOF closes that gap by refusing to teach them apart.

---

## 3 · CENTRAL CLAIM (1:00 – 1:30)

**Visual:** Six rounded cards in the "Six missions. One ascent." section fan in from the bottom. Per-mission accent colors visible as the cards build.

**Narration:**

> Six missions, one ascent. Each mission corresponds to a Bloom's cognitive level — Remember, Understand, Apply, Analyze, Evaluate, Create. Each mission asks for English in a different register, under a different kind of pressure, with a different professional role.
>
> The output is not a grade. The output is the **Pitch Capsule** — a five-panel professional artifact a learner can carry into a job interview.

---

## 4 · ONBOARDING WALK (1:30 – 2:45)

**Visual:** Click-through, 8 seconds per page:

1. **`/pages/signup.html`** — fill display name + email. Voice-guide card visible at top.
2. **`/pages/consent.html`** — bilingual PDPA consent. Hover the "what we do with this" expander. Toggle Thai/EN.
3. **`/pages/assessment.html`** — show 2–3 readiness items, the Thai language toggle, the "not a test" info alert.
4. **`/pages/avatar.html`** — webcam capture → AI stylized portrait appears.
5. **`/pages/mission-select.html`** — the 17-SDG color grid. Hover one tile. Click into a scenario.

**Narration (continuous through all five pages):**

> Onboarding takes about twelve minutes. We ask only what we need — your name, the institution, and a consent flow that's bilingual, granular, and reversible. Two checkboxes are required. Three are optional. You can change your mind any time.
>
> A short readiness check — not a test, but a calibration — sets the difficulty your missions will pace at. You build an operator avatar with a single photo, generated locally and deleted within twenty-four hours.
>
> Then you choose a goal. All seventeen, with three contextualised Thai scenarios each. Pick the one that pulls you in.

---

## 5 · A MISSION IN MOTION (2:45 – 4:30)

**Visual:** Mission shell with the cockpit layout open. Use the seeded **Burning Season** SDG-13 scenario for the demo.

- **Recon (Mission 01)** — show the LISTEN block playing an editor's voicemail; show the READ block surfacing two sources; click one to add it to the dossier; tokens animate +12.
- **Decode (Mission 02)** — show the SPEAK block recording a 30-second register-shift; AI judge gives formative inline feedback ("audience awareness: strong; hedging: weak").
- **Skip Deploy + Dissect** with a fast scroll past, narrating over.
- **Tribunal (Mission 05)** — show one round of the AI cross-examination. The integrity meter ticks on the right side. Tokens deduct on a weak answer.
- **Forge (Mission 06)** — open the Studio. Show the five-panel Pitch Capsule editor. Hit "Record narration" for two seconds. End on the panel checklist completing.

**Narration:**

> Inside a mission, four channels run in parallel — read, listen, speak, write — so language is never decoupled from the task. The Field Mentor is available, but it never gives you content answers. It asks Socratic questions. Your team is the source of truth.
>
> Earn Insight Tokens through good decisions; lose them through ethical blind spots. Spend them on scaffolding — an expert consultant, a pronunciation drill, a hint reveal — but never on the final judgment. That stays yours.
>
> In Tribunal, the platform's hardest mission, an AI cross-examines your reasoning under pressure. Your defense is in English. Your integrity score moves visibly. There is no neutral position.
>
> By Mission 06, the team enters the Studio and produces the Pitch Capsule — written in five registers, narrated in their own voice, designed to professional standards, exportable as PDF.

---

## 6 · PITCH CAPSULE + HALL (4:30 – 5:15)

**Visual:** Studio export → PDF preview → switch to `/pages/hall-of-excellence.html` showing three seeded capsules.

**Narration:**

> The Pitch Capsule is the deliverable. Five panels: The Crisis. The Journey. The Insight. The Solution. The Voice — narrated in the team's own English.
>
> Teams who consent can submit to the Hall of Excellence — a curated public gallery. The Hall is moderated by the teacher and reviewed by the platform. It travels beyond the classroom. It is portfolio-worthy.

---

## 7 · PEDAGOGICAL SPINE (5:15 – 5:45)

**Visual:** A clean static graphic — two parent frameworks (PICRAT, AI-TPACK) with the ten supporting frameworks branching beneath.

**Narration:**

> The platform sits on two underlying frameworks. **PICRAT** keeps every activity above Interactive-Amplifies. **AI-TPACK** draws the line between what AI handles — consistency, scale, formative feedback — and what only humans can — cultural authenticity, summative grading, voice recognition. The line is enforced in code, not in policy.

## Recording notes

- Record screens at native resolution (no upscaling artifacts).
- Cursor visible the whole time — turn on a subtle yellow halo cursor highlighter.
- All audio narration at -3 dB normalized; no music bed. Silence between blocks reads as confidence.
- Thai subtitles in IBM Plex Sans Thai 24 pt, white text with a 70% navy backdrop band.
- Export 1920×1080 H.264 .mp4, ≤ 200 MB.

---

## What this script deliberately doesn't do

- **No price / no GTM language.** This is academic.
- **No team biography.** The submission narrative carries that.
- **No "the AI thinks for you" framing.** Every voiceover line about AI is paired with a human-judgment line.
- **No marketing superlatives.** No "revolutionary," no "first-of-its-kind," no "game-changing."
- **No emojis** in any frame.
