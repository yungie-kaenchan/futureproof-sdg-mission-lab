# Teacher's Guide

**FUTUREPROOF: SDGs Mission Lab**
*v2-aligned (Thailand journey · Keystones · Voice for Change · reading
tiers). Thai translation of this guide is a submission deliverable.*

This guide is the practical manual for teachers running FUTUREPROOF in their classroom. It is structured around the natural cadence of a course term — before, during, and after — and assumes you have administrative access to the platform and a class roster ready to onboard.

---

## 1. Before the Course

### 1.1 What you'll do once

- Provision your class in **Admin Command → User Management → New Class**. Enter the class code (e.g. `ELT-301-A`), institution, and term identifier (e.g. `2026-T1`).
- Bulk-import your roster as a CSV with columns: `displayName`, `email`, `yearOfStudy`. The system will email each student a sign-up link.
- Confirm class settings:
  - **Language Mode** — `english` (default) or `english+thaiSupport` (lower-proficiency cohorts).
  - **Cognitive Pace Mode default** — off unless you have specific accommodations to honor for the cohort.
  - **Token Inflation** — leave at 1.0 for first-time runs.

### 1.2 What students will do once

Each student completes the onboarding in this **order** (the order is
the PDPA fix — consent precedes any personal-data collection):

1. **Platform Introduction** — how it works, the journey, how they're
   evaluated. Public, pre-sign-up.
2. **Sign up** — name, email, password, institution, year of study.
3. **PDPA consent** — bilingual flow with granular flags. The readiness
   check is **hard-gated**: it will not run and writes nothing to the
   cloud until consent is recorded. Students can update consent any time.
4. **Readiness check** — ~12–15 min diagnostic. Calibrates their
   `learnerProfile` **and assigns a reading tier** {1,2,3}.
5. **Score Report** — shows the learner their reading tier *and why
   audio is not tiered*. Adaptivity is made visible, not hidden.
6. **Operator avatar** — stylised portrait.
7. **The Thailand Journey** — a map of six regions; any-order entry.

You don't need to monitor onboarding live. Check Admin → Analytics the
next day to confirm everyone is through. **Note:** v2 replaced the v1
"pick an SDG → 3 generated scenarios" wheel with a single fixed journey
of six deep, vetted regional missions.

### 1.3 Team formation

You can either let students self-form (3 per team) or assign them. Assignments are made on **Admin → User Management → Teams**. Each team must have one of each role — Research Analyst, Communications Director, Ethics & Policy Officer. Solo and pair players are accommodated; the platform fills missing roles with NPC consultants.

## 2. Running the Journey (six regional missions)

In v2 there are **not** six different Bloom missions. There is **one
journey across six Thai regions**; learners take them in **any order**,
and **every** mission runs the same 5-stage mastery-spiral arc:

**BRIEF · PROBE · DECIDE · ACT · DEBRIEF**

Each mission is ~90–120 min live plus 1–2 h async. The pattern:

1. 10-minute briefing of the region's real trade-off and what the
   rubric values (no need to teach the SDG content — they'll work it).
2. Teams enter the mission; the dossier is served **at each student's
   reading tier** (same facts, different language). Audio/video is
   authentic for everyone, caption-scaffolded.
3. You coach between teams — not the answer key.
4. The Field Mentor is Socratic-only, in-app, never content.
5. Clearing the pass bar awards that region's **SDG Keystone**
   (idempotent, binary — it is *not* a grade). Six Keystones unlock the
   Voice for Change.

### 2.1 The two built missions (playable now) + the journey

Three regions are fully playable (Khon Kaen SDG 6, Chiang Mai SDG 13,
Bangkok SDG 11); the other three are documented to production-master
depth. Each runs the same arc — coach to the *stage*, not a Bloom level:

| Stage | Bloom | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| BRIEF | Remember/Understand | Do they grasp the tension *and* whose interests it pits? |
| PROBE | Analyze | Are they weighing source provenance, or trusting the loudest voice? |
| DECIDE | Evaluate | Is confidence honestly calibrated? (a confidence trap is deliberate) |
| ACT | Apply | Does the communication fit the audience it lands on? |
| DEBRIEF | Metacognition | Are they refining against the strongest counter, or strawmanning? |

**Pass / no dead-ends.** Below the bar, a learner may replay DECIDE/ACT;
or you may grant the Keystone for assessed offline-equivalent work
(**teacher override** — logged with your id + reason for the audit
trail). A **demo bypass** exists for judging only — double-gated and
off by default; do not enable it for a real cohort.

### 2.2 The Field Mentor

~10 mentor questions per mission. It cannot give answers. If a team is
stuck, ask them what they've tried — don't send them to the mentor.

### 2.3 Token-based scaffolding (distinct from Keystones)

**Insight Tokens** are the spendable process reward; **SDG Keystones**
are the non-spendable progression key. Tokens *cannot* buy a Keystone —
that separation protects the productive struggle. Tokens spend on:

- **Expert Consultant** (15) — one strategic AI question.
- **Language Coach Session** (10) — detailed AI feedback on a draft.
- **Hint Reveal** (5) — one hidden data point.
- **Decision Replay** (25) — redo one decision.
- **Pronunciation Drill** (10) — AI-guided practice. *(Also: every
  glossed dossier word has a one-tap pronunciation speaker, free.)*

Teams that spend strategically out-perform hoarders — surface this once
a cohort has a couple of missions behind them.

## 3. The Voice for Change (the capstone)

Unlocked by **six Keystones** (or teacher override / demo bypass). The
prompt: *having walked all six frontlines, propose one concrete action
for a Thai community — addressed to a real audience, not the teacher.*

Three submission lanes (CAST UDL — multiple means of expression; the
lane never changes the bar):

1. **Record live** — in-browser, 60–180 s, unlimited re-record.
2. **Upload** — a prepared audio or short video file.
3. **Create with Canva** — opens a template in a **new tab** (never
   embedded), exported and re-uploaded.

Most of your engagement is at the **Rubric A** scoring stage after
submission. Curation into the public **Hall of Voices** requires a
**second, explicit, withdrawable** consent (default off); optional
routing to a real Thai youth SDG forum needs a further opt-in. Grade the
argument and its expression — never reward an expensive lane over a
clearer cheaper one.

## 4. Grading

You are responsible for the **summative grade** — Tier 3 of the four-tier judging system. Your final grade is authoritative. AI Judge formative scores are advisory, useful for surfacing patterns, but never overturn your judgment.

The four rubrics (all updated for v2):

- **Rubric A** — Voice for Change holistic (you; the official grade).
- **Rubric B** — Mission decision quality across the 5-stage arc (AI
  formative; you see every decision in the dashboard).
- **Rubric C** — CEFR-aligned language development (pre/post; read the
  *delta*, not the absolute band — see the tier-fairness rule).
- **Rubric D** — Soft skills (you + peer judges, after a passed mission).

Suggested grade weighting:
- Voice for Change (Rubric A) — 50%
- Language growth (Rubric C) — 20%
- Soft skills (Rubric D) — 20%
- Mission decision aggregate (Rubric B audit) — 10%

You can re-weight on your syllabus. The Keystones are a *gate*, not a
grade — earning six unlocks the capstone; they are never averaged in.

## 5. The Admin Command Platform

Seven modules:

1. **User Management** — accounts, classes, teams.
2. **Mission Architect** — add/edit scenarios for special cohorts.
3. **Analytics Dashboard** — engagement, decision patterns by stage, language metrics, reading-tier distribution, Keystones earned, Voice for Change trends.
4. **Moderation** — review AI content; flag and replay if a team got an unfair-looking judgment.
5. **PDPA Center** — consent tracking, deletion requests, audit logs.
6. **System Configuration** — model selection, difficulty parameters.
7. **Hall of Voices Curator** — review submissions; publish only those with explicit, current consent (withdrawal removes them).

All data is exportable as CSV / PDF for institutional reporting.

## 6. After the Course

- Students can return to **My Data** at any time to export, correct, or delete their data.
- Personal data auto-deletes 90 days post-course.
- The 30-day reflection (Voice of the Learner / Time Capsule) is automated — students get an email, you don't need to do anything.

## 7. Common Issues

| Symptom | First check |
|---|---|
| Team stuck on a mission | Ask what they've tried, not what they need. Resist the urge to answer. |
| AI Judge feedback feels off | Open the decision in moderation; replay against the rubric. |
| A student withdraws photo consent mid-course | Their avatar reverts to placeholder; nothing else changes. |
| A student asks to delete account | Self-service in My Data; takes effect after 7-day grace. |
| Team stuck at DECIDE/DEBRIEF (the counter-position pushes hard) | It is *supposed* to. Reframe — "the model isn't graded, your reasoning is" — and point them to replay DECIDE rather than hand them the answer. |
| Learner anxious about their reading tier | Reassure: the tier only changes wording, never the facts, the decision, or the grade ceiling; it's fixed at entry and never punishes mid-mission. |
| A learner can't finish a region | Replay DECIDE/ACT, or use **teacher override** to grant the Keystone for assessed offline-equivalent work — no learner is dead-ended. |

## 8. The Pedagogical Spine

You are still the architect. The platform handles scale; it does not replace your judgment. Three reminders:

1. **Productive struggle is the point.** When students ask for the answer, they are also asking you to teach them to find one.
2. **The Field Mentor will not undermine you.** It is engineered to redirect to teammates and to you.
3. **Cultural authenticity is your domain.** The six missions are
   authored and vetted (real institutions for context, illustrative
   ranges never invented stats); you are the final arbiter of whether
   the Thai framing rings true for your cohort.
