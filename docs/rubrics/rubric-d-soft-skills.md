# Rubric D — Soft Skills Demonstration

**Used by:** Teacher Judges (summative reflection) + Peer Judges (cross-team, after a passed regional mission)
**Applies to:** the full Thailand journey, observable through decision logs, Field Mentor interactions, and the Voice for Change capstone
**Scale:** Each dimension scored 1 (Emerging) → 4 (Proficient) → 6 (Distinguished)

> v2 note: the "where to look" pointers below map to the 5-stage arc
> (BRIEF·PROBE·DECIDE·ACT·DEBRIEF) that every region mission runs, plus
> the Voice for Change. Dimensions and descriptors are unchanged — they
> are framework-level and v2-stable.

---

## Five Dimensions

### 1. Critical Thinking

| Score | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 6 | Routinely tests own reasoning; volunteers counter-examples to own claims; revises position when warranted. |
| 4 | Considers counter-examples when prompted; engages with feedback substantively. |
| 1 | Treats first reasoning as final reasoning; resists or ignores feedback. |

**Where to look:** DEBRIEF counterargument handling; DECIDE confidence-calibration; Field Mentor history (Socratic, never answers).

### 2. Analytical Thinking

| Score | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 6 | Decomposes problems into multi-axis comparisons; distinguishes signal from noise; weighs evidence with stated criteria. |
| 4 | Decomposes problems into recognizable parts; weighs evidence implicitly. |
| 1 | Treats problems as unitary; offers conclusions without analytic chain. |

**Where to look:** PROBE source-weighing across the dossier + four voices; DECIDE strategy rationale under the regional trade-off.

### 3. Collaboration

| Score | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 6 | Distinguishes own role from teammates' roles; visibly defers and visibly leads at appropriate moments; surfaces unspoken disagreements. |
| 4 | Plays role; contributes consistently; resolves disagreement when surfaced. |
| 1 | Either dominates or withdraws; collaboration is performative. |

**Where to look:** Decision log byUid distribution; Field Mentor "what does your teammate think?" redirects; role-led contributions across the arc and in the Voice for Change.

### 4. Ethical Reasoning

| Score | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 6 | Articulates ethical stakes proactively; engages with the strongest counterargument; distinguishes ethical claims from preferences. |
| 4 | Recognizes ethical stakes when prompted; engages with counterargument with effort. |
| 1 | Treats ethics as either obvious or irrelevant. |

**Where to look:** DECIDE/DEBRIEF ethical reasoning on the regional trade-off; the Voice for Change argument; documented AI-collaboration reflection.

### 5. Decision-Making Under Uncertainty

| Score | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 6 | Commits to a decision with stated trade-offs and reversibility analysis; updates the decision when new evidence demands it. |
| 4 | Commits to a decision with stated rationale. |
| 1 | Avoids committing; or commits without rationale. |

**Where to look:** DECIDE under the trade-off (incl. the deliberate confidence trap); DEBRIEF revision; the evidence commitment carried into the Voice for Change.

---

## Total

Maximum: 30 (5 × 6).

| Score band | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 25–30 | Distinguished — work is portfolio-ready in soft-skill demonstrations |
| 18–24 | Proficient — solid demonstration across most dimensions |
| 12–17 | Developing — uneven; one or two dimensions emerging |
| ≤ 11  | Emerging — recommend supplemental coaching |

---

## Use of Rubric D in Peer Judging (cross-team, mid-journey)

After a team has passed at least one regional mission, each team scores one peer team using Rubric D on that mission's decision trace. Peer scores feed metacognition — they are not summative grades. The teacher dashboard surfaces the variance between AI / Peer / Self / Teacher scores as a learning signal, not a calibration target.

---

## Notes

- This rubric is deliberately blind to native-language background and cultural style. Distinguished collaboration looks different in different cultural contexts; the descriptors above describe *visible behavior*, not personality.
- Where decision logs are insufficient to score a dimension, the teacher records "insufficient evidence" rather than a default low score. This is not penalty.
