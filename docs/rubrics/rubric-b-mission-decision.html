Mission Decision Quality — AI Judge Logic
รูบริก B · คุณภาพการตัดสินใจในภารกิจ (ตรรกะ AI Judge)
Purpose · วัตถุประสงค์
Make the AI Judges' scoring logic transparent and auditable — for teachers, learners, and the competition judges. Every decision a learner submits inside a mission is scored against the criteria in this rubric. The platform's feedback prompts and Insight-Token awards are deterministic functions of these scores.
Objectives · เป้าหมายเฉพาะ
  • Specify the exact dimensions on which AI Judges score each of the five mission stages (BRIEF → PROBE → DECIDE → ACT → DEBRIEF).
  • Anchor every AI prompt to descriptors a teacher could audit by hand on a random-sample basis.
  • Document the deliberate confidence-trap at the DECIDE stage and why it is pedagogically defensible.
  • Define how stage scores compose into the mission pass-threshold that awards an SDG Keystone (idempotent — keystones cannot be double-counted).
  • Specify the formative-feedback contract: every return includes a specific actionable growthEdge; a null fails validation and the call retries — the journey never blocks.
Applies To · ขอบเขตการใช้
Each decision logged during a mission. Six regional missions (EEC fringe health · Mae Sot education · Khon Kaen water · Bangkok cities · Chiang Mai climate · Andaman marine) all run the same 5-stage arc. The AI Judge scores per stage; the engine composes the mission pass-threshold from those scores.
Scoring Scale · มาตราการให้คะแนน
Each criterion 1 · 3 · 5. Insight Tokens awarded −10 … +25 from the integrated score (tokens are a process reward — they never buy a Keystone). Pass threshold: engine composite ≥ 0.60 OR net-token bar ≥ 8 ◆. Clearing the bar awards the SDG Keystone for that region (idempotent).
Used By · ผู้ใช้รูบริก
AI Judges (Tier 1 of the 4-tier hybrid judging system, formative, every mission, every region). Teachers audit randomly-sampled scores against this rubric to calibrate the model. The capstone (Voice for Change) is excluded by design — it belongs to Rubric A (teacher), preserving the AI-TPACK boundary.
Pedagogical Anchors · กรอบทฤษฎีที่ใช้
AI-TPACK (boundary) · PICRAT · Interactive-Amplifies · Bloom's Revised Taxonomy (Remember → Metacognition) · Hattie Visible Learning (formative feedback) · Vygotsky ZPD (the confidence-trap forces productive struggle)

Calibration & Operational Notes
  • AI-Judge calibration: during the pilot, random-sample ~5% of decisions and compare AI scores to a teacher's; drift greater than 0.5 score-point on any criterion triggers prompt revision.
  • growthEdge mandatory: every AI return must include a specific actionable growthEdge. A null fails validation and the call is retried — the journey never blocks.
  • No dead-ends: a learner below the bar may replay DECIDE/ACT, or a teacher may grant the Keystone for assessed offline-equivalent work (logged, audited — see Teacher Guide).
  • Composite pass rule: stage scores compose into the mission pass-threshold via the engine (default 0.60). Tokens reward process; the Keystone records "met the bar for this region."
