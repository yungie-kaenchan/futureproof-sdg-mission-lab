Voice for Change — Holistic Evaluation
รูบริก A · การประเมินผลงาน Voice for Change แบบองค์รวม
Purpose · วัตถุประสงค์
Provide the final, official summative grade for the learner's capstone artifact — the Voice for Change proposal — independent of which expression lane (live audio, uploaded audio/video, or Canva visual) the learner selected. Grade the argument and its expression, not the production polish of the lane. This rubric is the rubric the teacher uses; the AI does not grade the capstone — that boundary is the AI-TPACK principle made operational.
Objectives · เป้าหมายเฉพาะ
  • Measure language proficiency across academic, persuasive, and evaluative registers (C1 target for advanced LALA109 learners).
  • Surface critical and ethical reasoning quality — especially how the learner engages the strongest counter-position.
  • Assess craft of the chosen medium without privileging expensive production over a clearer cheaper one.
  • Verify synthesis of the evidence the learner committed to in-mission (the q6 commitment carried forward).
  • Document AI-collaboration ethics — what the learner did with AI vs. what they own as their own judgement.
Applies To · ขอบเขตการใช้
The learner's Voice for Change proposal — one concrete ask addressed to a real Thai community or institution — submitted in any one of three lanes (live audio recording · uploaded audio/video · Canva visual exported and uploaded). The lane is a means of expression (CAST UDL); it never changes the bar.
Scoring Scale · มาตราการให้คะแนน
Five dimensions, each scored 1 (Emerging) · 4 (Developing) · 7 (Proficient) · 10 (Distinguished). Dimensions 1–4 are weighted × 1.0; dimension 5 (AI Collaboration Ethics) × 0.5. Maximum: 45. Bands: 38–45 = A · 30–37 = B · 22–29 = C · ≤21 = Revise & Resubmit.
Used By · ผู้ใช้รูบริก
Teacher Judges (Tier 3 of the 4-tier hybrid judging system) — the human, summative, official grade. AI Judges produced formative feedback throughout the missions (Rubric B); peer judges scored soft skills (Rubric D); the capstone grade is human-only by design.

Rubric A · Voice for Change Holistic FUTUREPROOF · SDG MISSION JOURNEY
1 · Language Proficiency Across Registers · ×1.0
ScoreDescriptor
10 · DistinguishedControls academic, persuasive, and evaluative registers within one coherent piece. Hedging, modality, and metadiscourse used with intent. C1 alignment evident.
7 · ProficientGenerally appropriate register; occasional overreach on persuasion or under-specification on the evidence claim. B2 alignment evident.
4 · DevelopingRegister slips (the appeal reads academic; the evidence reads conversational). B1 alignment.
1 · EmergingRegister undifferentiated; errors interfere with meaning. A2 or below.
Look for Modal verbs in the policy ask; precise citation of the evidence committed in-mission; audience-appropriate affect when addressing the community. Tier-fair grading: judge proficiency against the learner's demonstrated control, not against native-likeness; a Tier-1 reader who argues precisely in plainer language is not penalised for plainness.
2 · Critical & Ethical Reasoning · ×1.0
ScoreDescriptor
10 · DistinguishedDefends a clear judgement that engages — not avoids — the strongest counter-position from the missions. The trade-off is named, not smoothed over. Reasoning chain explicit.
7 · ProficientPosition clear; the counter-position is acknowledged but treated thinly.
4 · DevelopingPosition stated, not defended; counter-position strawmanned or absent.
1 · EmergingNo discernible ethical reasoning; the conclusion is asserted.
Look for "however," "even so," "the strongest case against this is…"; explicit handling of the regional trade-off the learner walked (water vs. next generation, clean air vs. livelihood, flood defence vs. tenure, etc.).
3 · Craft of the Chosen Medium · ×1.0
ScoreDescriptor
10 · DistinguishedDelivery and structure reinforce the argument: paced narration · clean visual hierarchy · coherent edit — the medium serves the message as one artifact.
7 · ProficientStructure mostly clear; minor issues (uneven pacing, a redundant slide, a soft open).
4 · DevelopingStructure uneven; the audience has to work to follow the argument.
1 · EmergingNo discernible craft judgement in the chosen lane.
Lane-specific cues Audio — pacing, emphasis, audible structure. Video — shot/edit clarity, sound. Canva visual — hierarchy, restraint, type scale. Do not reward expensive production over a clearer cheaper one; a phone recording can score 10.
4 · Synthesis & the Evidence Commitment · ×1.0
ScoreDescriptor
10 · DistinguishedThe parts are inseparable; the proposal carries the evidence the learner committed to in a mission (the q6 commitment) — or argues explicitly why it no longer holds. The address to the audience resolves, not repeats.
7 · ProficientParts relate, but one is decorative; the evidence commitment is referenced but thinly used.
4 · DevelopingParts sequenced rather than synthesised; the prior evidence commitment is absent.
1 · EmergingParts do not connect.
Look for Does the proposed action follow from the crisis and the evidence, or just sit next to them? Cross-check the learner's recorded evidence-commitment surfaced on the Final Task screen.
5 · Documented AI Collaboration & Authorship Ethics · ×0.5
ScoreDescriptor
10 · DistinguishedAI use is named, contextualised, and reflected on; the learner states which judgements stayed their own.
7 · ProficientAI use named; reflection generic.
4 · DevelopingAI use acknowledged only in passing.
1 · EmergingAI use not acknowledged.
Look for The learner's stated boundary between AI-assisted language and learner-owned judgement — the same boundary the platform models.
Notes for the Grader
Score on evidence in the submission, not impression of effort or lane cost. The diagnostic reading tier is a fairness lens for dimension 1 only — it never lowers the bar for reasoning, synthesis, or ethics. A learner who reached the capstone has six Keystones; this grade evaluates the capstone itself, independently.
