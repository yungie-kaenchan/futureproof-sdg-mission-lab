# Rubric A — Pitch Capsule Holistic Evaluation

**Used by:** Teacher Judges (Tier 3, summative — final grade)
**Applies to:** Mission 06 FORGE artifact (the 5-panel Pitch Capsule + audio)
**Scale:** 1 (Emerging) → 4 (Developing) → 7 (Proficient) → 10 (Distinguished)
**Weighting:** Each dimension weighted equally unless the teacher has class-specific reasons to re-weight (declared on the syllabus).

---

## 1. Language Proficiency Across Registers (× 1.0)

| Score | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 10 | Demonstrates control of academic, persuasive, and evaluative registers across panels. Hedging, modality, and metadiscourse are used with intent. CEFR alignment with C1 evident. |
| 7  | Generally appropriate register per panel. Occasional overreach on persuasion or under-specification on academic claims. CEFR alignment with B2 evident. |
| 4  | Register slips frequently between panels. Persuasive panel reads academic; academic panel reads conversational. CEFR alignment with B1. |
| 1  | Register undifferentiated. Errors interfere with meaning. CEFR alignment with A2 or below. |

**Look for:** modal verbs in policy claims; citation grammar in evidence panel; affective vocabulary in solution panel.

---

## 2. Critical & Ethical Reasoning (× 1.0)

| Score | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 10 | Articulates and defends a clear ethical judgment that engages — not avoids — the strongest counterargument. Reasoning chain is explicit. |
| 7  | Ethical position is clear; counterargument is acknowledged but treated thinly. |
| 4  | Position stated but not defended. Counterarguments unaddressed or strawmanned. |
| 1  | No discernible ethical reasoning. Conclusion is asserted, not argued. |

**Look for:** the word "however," "even so," "the strongest case against this is…" — markers of genuine engagement.

---

## 3. Visual Design Judgment & Editorial Coherence (× 1.0)

| Score | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 10 | Layout and typography reinforce hierarchy. Visual rhythm across the five panels reads like a single artifact. |
| 7  | Hierarchy mostly clear. Minor inconsistencies in spacing, alignment, or palette use. |
| 4  | Hierarchy uneven. Reader has to work to follow the argument visually. |
| 1  | No discernible design judgment. |

**Look for:** consistent type scale; deliberate use of empty space; restraint in color use.

---

## 4. Synthesis (× 1.0)

| Score | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 10 | The five panels are inseparable — none could be removed without breaking the argument. The Voice panel resolves rather than repeats. |
| 7  | Panels relate, but one is decorative or redundant. |
| 4  | Panels feel sequenced rather than synthesized. |
| 1  | Panels do not connect. |

**Look for:** does the Insight panel earn the Solution panel? Does the Voice panel say something new?

---

## 5. Documented AI Collaboration & Authorship Ethics (× 0.5)

| Score | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 10 | AI use is named, contextualized, and reflected on. The team articulates which decisions remained their own. |
| 7  | AI use is named. Reflection on authorship is generic. |
| 4  | AI use is acknowledged only in passing. |
| 1  | AI use is not acknowledged. |

**Look for:** the team's stated boundary between AI-generated language and team-owned judgment.

---

## Total

Maximum: 45 (4 × 10 + 0.5 × 10)
Suggested grading bands:
- 38–45: A (distinguished)
- 30–37: B (proficient)
- 22–29: C (developing)
- ≤ 21: revise and resubmit

**Notes for the grader:** Score on evidence in the artifact, not your impression of effort. If two dimensions are tied to the same panel, score them independently. The Voice panel scores against (1), (4), and the synthesis half of (4) — not against design (3) directly.
