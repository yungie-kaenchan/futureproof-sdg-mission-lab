# Rubric A — Voice for Change Holistic Evaluation

**Used by:** Teacher Judges (Tier 3, summative — the final, official grade)
**Applies to:** the **Voice for Change** capstone — the learner's one
concrete proposal for a real Thai community, submitted in **any one of
three lanes** (live audio recording · uploaded audio/video · a Canva
visual exported and uploaded). The lane is a means of expression (CAST
UDL); it never changes the bar.
**Why a teacher grades this (not the AI):** every mission that led here
was AI-scaffolded for consistency and scale. The summative judgement of a
human voice stays with a human — this is the AI-TPACK boundary made
operational. Grade the *argument and its expression*, not the production
polish of the lane.
**Scale:** 1 (Emerging) → 4 (Developing) → 7 (Proficient) → 10 (Distinguished)
**Weighting:** dimensions 1–4 × 1.0; dimension 5 × 0.5 (declare any
class-specific re-weighting on the syllabus).

> v2 note: this replaces the v1 "5-panel Pitch Capsule" rubric. There are
> no fixed panels — judge the proposal's *parts* (the crisis, the one
> action, who it helps, the evidence, the address to the audience),
> however the learner's chosen lane carries them.

---

## 1. Language Proficiency Across Registers (× 1.0)

| Score | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 10 | Controls academic, persuasive, and evaluative registers within one coherent piece. Hedging, modality, and metadiscourse used with intent. C1 alignment evident. |
| 7  | Generally appropriate register; occasional overreach on persuasion or under-specification on the evidence claim. B2 alignment evident. |
| 4  | Register slips (the appeal reads academic; the evidence reads conversational). B1 alignment. |
| 1  | Register undifferentiated; errors interfere with meaning. A2 or below. |

**Look for:** modal verbs in the policy ask; precise citation of the
evidence committed in-mission; audience-appropriate affect when
addressing the community. *Tier-fair grading:* judge proficiency against
the learner's demonstrated control, not against native-likeness; a
Tier-1 reader who argues precisely in plainer language is not penalised
for plainness.

---

## 2. Critical & Ethical Reasoning (× 1.0)

| Score | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 10 | Defends a clear judgement that engages — not avoids — the strongest counter-position from the missions. The trade-off is named, not smoothed over. Reasoning chain explicit. |
| 7  | Position clear; the counter-position is acknowledged but treated thinly. |
| 4  | Position stated, not defended; counter-position strawmanned or absent. |
| 1  | No discernible ethical reasoning; the conclusion is asserted. |

**Look for:** "however," "even so," "the strongest case against this
is…"; explicit handling of the regional trade-off the learner walked
(water vs. next generation, clean air vs. livelihood, flood defence vs.
tenure, etc.).

---

## 3. Craft of the Chosen Medium (× 1.0)

| Score | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 10 | Delivery and structure reinforce the argument: paced narration / clean visual hierarchy / coherent edit — the medium serves the message as one artifact. |
| 7  | Structure mostly clear; minor issues (uneven pacing, a redundant slide, a soft open). |
| 4  | Structure uneven; the audience has to work to follow the argument. |
| 1  | No discernible craft judgement in the chosen lane. |

**Lane-specific cues:** *audio* — pacing, emphasis, audible structure;
*video* — shot/edit clarity, sound; *Canva visual* — hierarchy,
restraint, type scale. Do **not** reward expensive production over a
clearer cheaper one; a phone recording can score 10.

---

## 4. Synthesis & the Evidence Commitment (× 1.0)

| Score | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 10 | The parts are inseparable; the proposal carries the **evidence the learner committed to in a mission** (the q6 commitment) — or argues explicitly why it no longer holds. The address to the audience resolves, not repeats. |
| 7  | Parts relate, but one is decorative; the evidence commitment is referenced but thinly used. |
| 4  | Parts sequenced rather than synthesised; the prior evidence commitment is absent. |
| 1  | Parts do not connect. |

**Look for:** does the proposed action *follow from* the crisis and the
evidence, or just sit next to them? Cross-check the learner's recorded
evidence-commitment surfaced on the Final Task screen.

---

## 5. Documented AI Collaboration & Authorship Ethics (× 0.5)

| Score | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 10 | AI use is named, contextualised, and reflected on; the learner states which judgements stayed their own. |
| 7  | AI use named; reflection generic. |
| 4  | AI use acknowledged only in passing. |
| 1  | AI use not acknowledged. |

**Look for:** the learner's stated boundary between AI-assisted language
and learner-owned judgement — the same boundary the platform models.

---

## Total

Maximum: **45** (4 × 10 + 0.5 × 10). Suggested bands:
- 38–45: A (distinguished) · 30–37: B (proficient) · 22–29: C (developing) · ≤ 21: revise & resubmit.

**Notes for the grader:** score on evidence in the submission, not
impression of effort or lane cost. The diagnostic reading tier is a
*fairness lens for dimension 1 only* — it never lowers the bar for
reasoning, synthesis, or ethics. A learner who reached the capstone has
six Keystones; this grade evaluates the capstone itself, independently.
