รูบริก D · การแสดงทักษะ Soft Skills ทั้ง ๕ มิติ
Purpose · วัตถุประสงค์
Surface and grade the five soft skills the platform is designed to cultivate. Unlike a language rubric, this measures visible behaviour observable in the decision log and Mr Compass interaction history. It is deliberately blind to native-language background and cultural style; distinguished collaboration looks different in different cultures, and the descriptors describe behaviour, not personality.
Objectives · เป้าหมายเฉพาะ
  • Score each learner on five framework-stable dimensions: Critical Thinking · Analytical Thinking · Collaboration · Ethical Reasoning · Decision-Making Under Uncertainty.
  • Anchor every score to specific evidence sources in the platform — every dimension has a 'where to look' pointer that is auditable.
  • Enable Peer Judging (Tier 2) — give peer teams a usable instrument to score each other after a passed regional mission.
  • Surface AI / Peer / Self / Teacher score variance on the Admin Dashboard as a learning signal, not a calibration target.
  • Where evidence is insufficient, allow the teacher to record 'insufficient evidence' rather than a default low score — this is not a penalty.
Applies To · ขอบเขตการใช้
The full Thailand journey — observable through decision logs, Mr Compass interaction history, peer-team observation, and the Voice for Change capstone reflection. Six regional missions provide six observation windows.
Scoring Scale · มาตราการให้คะแนน
Each dimension scored 1 (Emerging) · 4 (Proficient) · 6 (Distinguished). Maximum: 30 (5 × 6). Bands: 25–30 Distinguished · 18–24 Proficient · 12–17 Developing · ≤ 11 Emerging.
Used By · ผู้ใช้รูบริก
Teacher Judges (summative reflection at end of course) + Peer Judges (Tier 2, cross-team, after a passed regional mission). Peer scores feed metacognition — they are not summative grades.

1 · Critical Thinking
ScoreDescriptor
6 · DistinguishedRoutinely tests own reasoning; volunteers counter-examples to own claims; revises position when warranted.
4 · ProficientConsiders counter-examples when prompted; engages with feedback substantively.
1 · EmergingTreats first reasoning as final reasoning; resists or ignores feedback.
Where to look DEBRIEF counterargument handling; DECIDE confidence-calibration; Mr Compass interaction history (Socratic, never answers).
2 · Analytical Thinking
ScoreDescriptor
6 · DistinguishedDecomposes problems into multi-axis comparisons; distinguishes signal from noise; weighs evidence with stated criteria.
4 · ProficientDecomposes problems into recognizable parts; weighs evidence implicitly.
1 · EmergingTreats problems as unitary; offers conclusions without analytic chain.
Where to look PROBE source-weighing across the dossier + four stakeholder voices; DECIDE strategy rationale under the regional trade-off.
3 · Collaboration
ScoreDescriptor
6 · DistinguishedDistinguishes own role from teammates' roles; visibly defers and visibly leads at appropriate moments; surfaces unspoken disagreements.
4 · ProficientPlays role; contributes consistently; resolves disagreement when surfaced.
1 · EmergingEither dominates or withdraws; collaboration is performative.
Where to look Decision-log byUid distribution; Mr Compass "what does your teammate think?" redirects; role-led contributions across the arc and in the Voice for Change.
4 · Ethical Reasoning
ScoreDescriptor
6 · DistinguishedArticulates ethical stakes proactively; engages with the strongest counterargument; distinguishes ethical claims from preferences.
4 · ProficientRecognizes ethical stakes when prompted; engages with counterargument with effort.
1 · EmergingTreats ethics as either obvious or irrelevant.
Where to look DECIDE / DEBRIEF ethical reasoning on the regional trade-off; the Voice for Change argument; documented AI-collaboration reflection.
5 · Decision-Making Under Uncertainty
ScoreDescriptor
6 · DistinguishedCommits to a decision with stated trade-offs and reversibility analysis; updates the decision when new evidence demands it.
4 · ProficientCommits to a decision with stated rationale.
1 · EmergingAvoids committing; or commits without rationale.
Where to look DECIDE under the trade-off (incl. the deliberate confidence trap); DEBRIEF revision; the evidence commitment carried into the Voice for Change.
Peer Judging (Tier 2) and Variance as Learning Signal
After a team has passed at least one regional mission, each team scores one peer team using Rubric D on that mission's decision trace. Peer scores feed metacognition — they are not summative grades. The Admin Dashboard surfaces the variance between AI · Peer · Self · Teacher scores as a learning signal, not a calibration target. Cultural-style blindness is intentional: distinguished collaboration looks different in different cultural contexts; the descriptors above describe visible behavior, not personality.
Insufficient-Evidence Rule
Where decision logs are insufficient to score a dimension (e.g., a learner exempted from a particular stage), the teacher records "insufficient evidence" rather than defaulting to a low score. This is not a penalty — it is honest accounting.
