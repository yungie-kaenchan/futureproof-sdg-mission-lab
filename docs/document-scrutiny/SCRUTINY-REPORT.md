The rule: *"รายงานนวัตกรรมไม่เกิน 15 หน้า"* — interpreted conservatively, this includes the full submitted PDF. We are 1 page over.

**Alternative if pages-as-PDF-rule is interpreted strictly:** reduce the line-spacing of the Three-Theory Mapping block from 1.05 → 1.00 just for that one block; that alone may pull the document back to 15. Less editorial work than rewriting.

### 2.2 · HARD: No reference list / no in-text citations

Every paragraph in §3.1 cites a framework by author name *without a year and without a reference*. At the doctoral-academic level SPU judges represent (M.Ed. faculty), this is the single biggest weakness. The fix is mechanical: add an APA-7 reference list at the end of the document (which can spill onto the final signature page rather than count toward the 15-page limit if formatted as appendix), and insert minimal in-text year-references (`Mishra & Koehler, 2006`, etc.) inline.

Full proposed bibliography in §4 below. The complete list runs to ~ 28 sources — a defensible academic-paper quantity for a 15-page innovation report.

### 2.3 · SOFT: Thai language and consistency

Approximately 30 specific issues, listed in §3 below. None individually fatal; collectively they cost polish.
