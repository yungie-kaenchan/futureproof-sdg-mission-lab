The Adaptive Asymmetry — visible, fair, and SLA-correct

Reading text → tiered (3 registers)

The diagnostic sets a CEFR-mapped reading tier. Every dossier is pre-authored at three registers — identical facts, stakeholders and decision; only the language changes. Self-paced reading must meet the learner at i+1 or comprehension collapses.

◆ Tier 1 A2–B1 · Tier 2 B1+–B2 · Tier 3 B2+–C1

Audio / video → NOT tiered

Authentic input is held constant. Time-bound listening benefits from controlled stretch because the caption (.vtt) scaffold exists. Tiering text while holding audio constant is the SLA-correct asymmetry — not a production shortcut.

◆ Krashen i+1 · Mayer dual coding

The learner is told this on their Score Report. Adaptivity is surfaced as a fairness statement — not hidden as a difficulty knob.
